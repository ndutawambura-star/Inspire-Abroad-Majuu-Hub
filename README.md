# Book Sales Page

A clean, responsive, and high-converting landing page template designed for authors and publishers to showcase and sell books directly to readers.

---

## 📖 Features

•⁠  ⁠*Hero Section:* Large book cover display, subtitle, and primary call-to-action (CTA) button.
•⁠  ⁠*Author Bio:* Dedicated section to build credibility and connect with readers.
•⁠  ⁠*Chapter Breakdown:* Clear overview of key topics or chapters.
•⁠  ⁠*Testimonials & Reviews:* Social proof section for reader reviews, press quotes, or expert endorsements.
•⁠  ⁠*Pricing & Buy Options:* Support for single books, bundles, or digital vs. physical formats.
•⁠  ⁠*Mobile-First Design:* Optimized for seamless viewing on phones, tablets, and desktops.

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

•⁠  ⁠*HTML5:* Semantic structure for optimal SEO and accessibility.
•⁠  ⁠*CSS3 / Tailwind CSS:* Custom styling and responsive layout.
•⁠  ⁠*JavaScript (Vanilla):* Light interactive elements (smooth scrolling, mobile menu).

## ⚙️ Customization Guide

1.⁠ ⁠*Book Details:* Edit ⁠ index.html ⁠ to update the book title, subtitle, description, and pricing.
2.⁠ ⁠*Cover Image:* Replace ⁠ assets/images/book-cover.jpg ⁠ with your own 3D or flat cover graphic.
3.⁠ ⁠*Payment Links:* Update the ⁠ href ⁠ on the "Buy Now" buttons with your payment provider link (e.g., Stripe, Gumroad, Amazon, or Lemon Squeezy).
4.⁠ ⁠*Styling:* Adjust colors and typography in ⁠ styles.css ⁠ to match your brand.
