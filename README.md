# 🌍 Inspire Abroad — Majuu Hub

### Scholarships • Global Mobility • Community • Belonging • Storytelling

**Inspire Abroad — Majuu Hub** is an early-stage digital product created by **Jane Nduta Wambura** to bring together three parts of the international mobility journey:

> **Find the opportunity. Build the life. Find your people.**

The project explores how technology can help African students discover international education opportunities while creating space for Africans abroad to connect, share experiences and have honest conversations about life between worlds.

At the heart of the project is ***Majuu: Life Between Two Worlds — Book One***, Jane's book and a starting point for conversations about migration, identity, ambition, adaptation and belonging.

---

## ✨ Why this project exists

Finding a scholarship is often treated as the finish line.

It isn't.

For many African students, the journey looks more like:

**Opportunity → Application → Scholarship → Relocation → Work → Community → Belonging**

The information needed at each stage is often scattered across university websites, scholarship portals, social media, WhatsApp groups, personal networks and lived experience.

**Inspire Abroad** is an attempt to bring those pieces closer together.

The long-term vision is a digital ecosystem where African students and professionals can:

* 🎓 Discover credible international education opportunities
* 🧭 Understand what happens after getting the opportunity
* 🌍 Navigate life and work abroad
* 🤝 Find community
* 💬 Exchange lived experiences
* 📖 Explore stories about identity and belonging
* 🚀 Turn international mobility into long-term opportunity

# 👩🏾‍💻 About the Founder

## Jane Nduta Wambura

Jane is a Kenyan global programme and product leader whose career has crossed international education, technology, project and programme management, community building and digital work.

Her own international education journey began with an **Erasmus Mundus scholarship** and an MSc in Clinical Nutrition at the University of Porto.

That experience became more than an academic journey. It exposed her to the realities of adapting to different countries, learning new systems, building international networks and developing a career across borders.

She later moved into roles involving technology, project management, business analysis, implementation and digital products, while continuing to work around international education and community.

Jane has also contributed to the **Erasmus Mundus Association**, including communications, IT, alumni engagement and international community-building work.

Her experience is the foundation for Inspire Abroad:

> **This is not a platform being built from a distance. It is being built from lived experience.**

Clone the repository:

```bash
git clone https://github.com/ndutawambura-star/Inspire-Abroad-Majuu-Hub.git
```

Enter the project:


# 🧪 Current Status

### Version 0.1 — Concept Prototype

The current release focuses on:

* Product storytelling
* Responsive interface
* Scholarship discovery UX
* Community positioning
* Majuu storytelling
* Founder narrative
* Initial information architecture

This is **not yet a production scholarship database**.

The scholarship entries currently included are prototype content and should not be treated as live application opportunities.

Before production, every opportunity should be connected to an authoritative source and include:

* Official scholarship URL
* Eligibility
* Deadline
* Funding information
* Study level
* Destination
* Field of study
* Last verified date

# 🔐 Responsible Technology

As the platform develops, trust and safety will be central.

Potential production requirements include:

* Verified scholarship sources
* Transparent recommendation logic
* Privacy-conscious user profiles
* Secure authentication
* Protection of application information
* Clear separation between information and professional immigration/legal advice
* Accessible design
* Responsible use of AI

The goal is not simply to make opportunity discovery faster.

It is to make it **more trustworthy**.
